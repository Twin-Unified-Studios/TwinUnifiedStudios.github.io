Bro FAQ

The useful stuff, without a scavenger hunt.

What is Bro?

Bro is a personality-driven productivity app for capturing, organizing, breaking down, and remembering tasks.

What is Break It Down?

It turns a larger task into smaller steps you can complete over time without losing your place.

What are personalities?

Normal, Casual, Gen Z, and Unhinged change how Bro communicates. They do not change the underlying task functionality.

What is Unhinged?

It is Bro’s most energetic, chaotic personality. Public-facing examples stay playful and brand-safe.

Is stronger language required?

No. Stronger language is optional, disabled by default, and not required for any personality or feature. If you enable it, some personalities may occasionally use profanity or other language that some people may find inappropriate. You can turn it off at any time. Users under the age of majority where they live should ask a parent or legal guardian before enabling it.

How do reminders work?

Bro supports task timing concepts including due reminders, earlier reminders, overdue status, and completion handling. Notifications must be enabled on your device for alerts to appear.

Does Bro support recurring tasks?

Yes. Bro is designed for daily, weekly, and other repeating responsibilities.

Does Bro support important dates?

Yes. Important Dates can help track birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, and other dates that matter.

What happens if notifications are disabled?

Tasks remain in the app, but the device cannot display Bro’s notification alerts. Re-enable notifications in system settings.

Does Bro require an account, and where is data stored?

Bro does not require an account. Your tasks, name, birthdays, important dates, preferences, and other information you enter are stored on your device and are not shared with Twin Unified Studios LLC.

Does Bro sync between devices?

No. Bro does not sync tasks or other app information between devices. Information entered on one device remains on that device.

What devices are supported?

Bro supports compatible iPhone devices running iOS 17.6 or later. See the App Store listing for the latest device compatibility details and to pre-order Bro now.

How old do I need to be to use Bro?

You must be at least 13 years old to use Bro. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you should use Bro only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. Bro is not directed to children under 13.

How do I report a bug?

Use Bro’s built-in Bug Report feature. It opens your email service with diagnostic details for your device type, operating-system version, and Bro app version. It does not automatically include tasks, names, birthdays, important dates, or other personal app information. See Bro Support for more help.
