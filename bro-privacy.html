Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Bro and Twin Unified Studios LLC handle information when you use the Bro app, browse the Bro website, or voluntarily send a bug report.

No account required

Bro does not require you to create or sign in to an account. Twin Unified Studios LLC does not receive an account profile because Bro does not create one.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter in Bro is stored on your device. This may include tasks, task notes, reminders, completion status, recurring tasks, preferences, your name, birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, and other important dates you choose to enter.

Bro does not transmit this app information to Twin Unified Studios LLC. Names, tasks, birthdays, important dates, and other personal content stored in Bro are not shared with us.

No synchronization between devices

Bro does not provide cross-device synchronization. Information entered on one device remains on that device and is not automatically copied to another phone, tablet, computer, or online account.

Age requirements

You must be at least 13 years old to use Bro. Bro is not directed to children under 13, and Twin Unified Studios does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13 through Bro or this website.

If you are under the legal age of majority where you live, you should review this policy with a parent or legal guardian and use Bro only with their permission. A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 has sent information to Twin Unified Studios should contact us so the matter can be reviewed and the information can be deleted when appropriate.

Bro’s App Store age rating may describe the suitability of the app’s content and may vary by region. That store rating is separate from the minimum age requirement stated in this policy.

This website

The Bro pages use the Twin Unified Studios theme and policy-preference storage in your browser. They do not add analytics, advertising pixels, user accounts, or a backend. If you contact support by email, the information you send is used to respond to your request.

The website stores limited preferences in your browser, including theme and Bro website personality choices. These preferences remain on your device unless you clear the site’s browser data. The website does not require you to provide a name, birthdate, or account to browse its public pages.

Notifications and task data

Bro uses device notification features to present reminders you configure. Task and reminder information remains on your device and is not sent to Twin Unified Studios LLC for notification delivery.

Notification permission is controlled through your device. You can change or disable that permission in system settings. Disabling notifications may prevent reminders from appearing but does not remove your tasks from the app.

Voluntary bug reports

Bro’s built-in Bug Report feature opens your device’s email service and prepares an email containing limited diagnostic information: your device type, operating-system version, and Bro app version. The report is not sent unless you choose to send it.

Bro does not automatically include your tasks, name, birthdays, important dates, reminders, task notes, or other personal app information in a bug report. Your email service may include your sending email address, and anything you manually add to the message will be shared when you send it. Please do not add sensitive task or personal information to a bug report.

Diagnostic details and any message you voluntarily send are used to investigate the reported problem, respond when appropriate, and improve Bro’s reliability.

Retention and deletion

Browser preferences can be removed by clearing this site’s data in your browser. App information can be changed or deleted using Bro’s available controls on the device. Because Bro does not provide cross-device sync, deleting Bro or losing access to the device may also remove access to information stored there.

Bug-report emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain service records, prevent abuse, improve reliability, or meet legal obligations.

Email services and legal requirements

When you choose to send a bug report, your selected email service processes and delivers that email under its own terms and privacy policy. Twin Unified Studios LLC may preserve or disclose a received bug report when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users, investigate misuse, or defend the rights and security of the app and company.

Security

App information is protected by the security of your device. You are responsible for controlling access to your device and keeping its operating system and security settings current. No device or electronic communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so avoid including sensitive personal information in a bug report email.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Bro’s features or practices change. The date at the top of this page will be changed when revisions are published. If a change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, Twin Unified Studios LLC will provide it through an appropriate channel.

Contact

Questions can be sent through Bro Support or to info@twinunifiedstudios.com.
