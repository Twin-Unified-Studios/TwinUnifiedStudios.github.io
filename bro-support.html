Bro Support

Something acting weird? Start with the quick checks below. If it still won’t cooperate, tell us what happened.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Close and reopen Bro, then try the action again.
  • Check for an app update and install the latest supported operating-system update. Bro requires iOS 17.6 or later.
  • Confirm the task’s due time, reminder time, and completion state.
  • Bro stores information on your device and does not sync between devices. Deleting the app may remove access to that on-device information.

Notification troubleshooting

  • Open your device’s system settings and confirm notifications are allowed for Bro.
  • Check Focus, Do Not Disturb, notification summaries, and device-level scheduling settings.
  • Confirm the task is not already completed and its reminder time has not passed.

Report a bug

Use Bro’s built-in Bug Report feature. It opens your email service and prepares a message containing only:

  • Your device type.
  • Your operating-system version.
  • Your Bro app version.

The report does not automatically include tasks, names, birthdays, important dates, reminders, notes, or other personal app information. You decide whether to send the email. Your email service may include your sending address, and anything you manually add will also be included, so do not add sensitive information.

Contact Bro Support

If you cannot or prefer not to use the in-app Bug Report feature, use this form. The diagnostic fields below are required so we can understand the environment where the problem occurred.

Before attaching anything, remove or obscure all personal information. Check Bro content, names, tasks, dates, notes, notification previews, and anything else visible on screen.

Your reply email, diagnostics, message, and optional attachment are sent through FormSubmit to Twin Unified Studios LLC. See the Bro Privacy Policy before sending. If the form fails to submit, email info@twinunifiedstudios.com instead and include your Bro version, iOS version, and iPhone model.

Known Issues

These issues are known, are being worked on, and are planned to be addressed in a future release.

  • Bro may report that it has crashed after you have been away from the app for a few hours. This does not delete or wipe any tasks.
  • A Break It Down list cannot be edited after it has been created.

Note: These known issues may affect parts of the app experience, but they are not known to delete or modify your tasks or other stored data. If you experience data loss or another serious issue, please report it as soon as possible.
