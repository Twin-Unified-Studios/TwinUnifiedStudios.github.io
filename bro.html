Easy to start. Easy to come back to.

Capture what needs doing, give it the detail it deserves, and keep moving without turning task management into another task.

What makes Bro, Bro?

The same practical task tools, delivered in a voice you choose—from straightforward to delightfully chaotic.

Break It Down

Big task? Break it down.

Turn something intimidating into smaller, resumable steps. Finish a piece, pause, and pick up where you left off.

  • Turn big tasks into smaller steps
  • Pause and return without losing your place
  • See your progress as you go

Personality

Your tasks. Your vibe.

Choose Normal, Casual, Gen Z, or brand-safe Unhinged. The voice changes; the useful task tools stay the same.

  • Pick from four distinct personalities
  • Change Bro's voice whenever you want
  • Keep the same practical task tools in every mode

Celebrations

Bro recognizes every completion.

Finish early, right on time, or after the deadline—Normal mode simply confirms that the task is complete.

  • Get extra credit for finishing early
  • Celebrate an on-time completion
  • Finish late without losing the win

Quick Add

Do this often?

Save common task setups and bring them back quickly, with less tapping and less repeated setup.

  • Save task setups you use often
  • Add familiar tasks with fewer taps
  • Spend less time repeating setup

Recurring Tasks

Some things come back.

Keep daily, weekly, and other repeating responsibilities from slipping through the cracks.

  • Repeat tasks on the schedule you need
  • Keep routines visible and ready
  • Complete one occurrence without losing the next

Important Dates

Remember the important stuff.

Keep birthdays, anniversaries, holidays, and other dates visible alongside everything else that matters.

  • Track birthdays and anniversaries
  • Remember holidays and personal milestones
  • Keep important dates beside your tasks

Organization

Find what matters.

Move between Today, Upcoming, Overdue, Completed, and Recurring views, then use priorities and markers to focus.

  • See what needs attention today
  • Move between upcoming, overdue, and completed work
  • Use priorities and markers to focus
