Product support

InvoBaseOS Support

Start with the checks below. If the problem continues, send a focused bug or user-experience report with the required app and device details.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Close and reopen InvoBaseOS, then repeat the action.
  • Install the latest available InvoBaseOS update.
  • Confirm your iPhone or iPad is running a supported operating-system version.
  • Record the exact steps that caused the issue and any message displayed.

Local-only information

InvoBaseOS information is stored locally on each device and does not synchronize between iPhone and iPad. Twin Unified Studios LLC cannot remotely view, retrieve, or repair your local invoices, customer records, credentials, or application data.

Important: Fully deleting InvoBaseOS can permanently remove its locally stored information. Twin Unified Studios LLC cannot recover data after the app and its local data have been deleted. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step unless you understand and accept that risk.

Report a bug or experience issue

Use this form for reproducible bugs, confusing behavior, accessibility problems, and other product-experience issues. FormSubmit’s standard CAPTCHA remains enabled.

Before attaching anything, remove or obscure customer names, addresses, contact details, invoice numbers, prices, tax information, payment information, credentials, business records, notification previews, and all other personal or confidential information.

Your email, diagnostics, message, and optional attachment are sent through FormSubmit to Twin Unified Studios LLC. Review the Privacy Policy before sending. If the form fails to submit, email info@twinunifiedstudios.com instead and include your InvoBaseOS version, device model, platform, and operating-system version.
